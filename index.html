How I build构建理念

i.

The machine is the source of truth设备是唯一的事实来源

If a sensor can know it, a person shouldn't have to type it. Data entry is a cost, not a feature.传感器能知道的事，就不该让人手动输入。数据录入是成本，不是功能。

ii.

Offline is the normal case离线才是常态

Factory networks drop. Every system buffers, retries and tells the truth about its gaps.工厂网络随时可能中断。每个系统都会缓存、重试，并如实记录自己的数据缺口。

iii.

Derived, never asserted只推导，不断言

Counts, verdicts and attendance are replayed from immutable events, so every number can be explained.产量、判定结果与出席状态，都由不可变的事件重放得出，因此每个数字都有据可查。

iv.

Unknown is a valid answer“未知”也是合格的答案

I don't guess a protocol, a tolerance or a business rule. I measure it, or I write down that it's open.协议、公差、业务规则，我都不靠猜。要么实测，要么明确记录为待定事项。
